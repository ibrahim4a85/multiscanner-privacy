Multi Scanner Tools Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Summary

Multi Scanner processes your documents entirely on your phone. We do not collect, store or share your documents or the text in them. There is no account, no cloud upload and no advertising.

Your documents

Scanned pages, recognized text, titles, folders and expiry dates are saved only in Multi Scanner's private storage on your device. Text recognition and document detection run on-device. Your documents leave your phone only when you choose to share them, for example as a PDF through the system share sheet.

Camera and photos

Multi Scanner's scanning camera takes photos only when you press the shutter. Edge detection, cropping, filters and clean-up run on your phone, and the photos are stored only in Multi Scanner's private storage. If you choose Google's scanner in settings, it runs on-device through Google Play services and Multi Scanner receives only the finished page images. Importing from your gallery happens only when you choose to do so. Multi Scanner never records audio.

Translation and QR codes

Translation runs on your device with Google ML Kit. The first time you use a language, its translation model is downloaded from Google; the text you translate is never sent. QR codes and barcodes are read with Google's code scanner in Google Play services, which processes the camera image on your device and gives Multi Scanner only the code's content. Recent scanned codes are kept on your phone until you clear them.

Notifications

Expiry reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No reminder data is sent to any server.

Diagnostics

The on-device text recognition, translation and code scanning features are provided by Google ML Kit. To keep these features working, ML Kit sends Google technical diagnostics and usage data: device and app information, a per-installation identifier, performance metrics, feature versions and error codes. This data never includes your documents, images or text, is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Purchases

The optional one-time Multi Scanner Pro purchase is processed by Google Play. Multi Scanner does not receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete any document inside the app, or uninstall Multi Scanner to remove all of its data from your device.
